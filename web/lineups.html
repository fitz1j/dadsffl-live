Lineup Report

Who is starting the right players — and who is leaving points on the bench. Every team's actual lineup against the best legal lineup its roster could have played that week.

How it's measured. Scored is what each team's starters earned. Best possible is the highest-scoring legal lineup from that week's 24-man roster, picked with hindsight: 1 K, 1 QB, 3 RB, 1 TE, 3 WR (an extra TE may fill a WR spot), 1 DL/LB, 1 DB. Efficiency is scored ÷ best possible; the gap is points left on the bench. Hindsight is a hard grader — kicker and defensive picks are mostly luck — so read small differences as noise.

Starters: the commissioner's weekly stats workbook (Wkly Player Tab), source …, added each week once his sheet is posted. Rosters: the same week-by-week rosters as the weekly head-to-head page (pre-season rosters plus the waiver wire), read from that page so the two never drift apart. Points: the league's scoring engine on ESPN box scores, from the same Supabase feed as the live scoring page — every player, starters and bench alike, is scored the same way.
