Post-Draft Rosters

All eight rosters exactly as they came off the commissioner's draft board, 24 players apiece, with each player's NFL team and bye week.

Where this comes from

Parsed from the Draft tab of the commissioner's live workbook (2026draftrosters090626 live.xlsx, updated 9/6/26) — 256 numbered slots, 192 real picks across 32 rounds, 64 “Roster Full” no-ops, every team exactly 24, no duplicate players. Bye weeks come from the bye chart inside that same workbook.

The draft board records no NFL team, so each player's team is joined from three sources in order: the handful of picks that carry a (TEAM) note in the cell, then a verified 2026 player-team map, then the workbook's own roster tabs. Two of the workbook's entries were left out in favour of the verified value: Maxx Crosby (sheet says BAL — the Raiders–Ravens trade was called off, he is still a Raider) and Rachaad White (sheet says TB — he signed with Washington in March). Everything else agrees.
